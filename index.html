How ReliefConnect Works

One platform connects people requesting help with volunteers, NGOs and available resources.

01
Report a Need

Citizens can submit a disaster-relief request with location, help type and urgency.

02
Priority is Calculated

A transparent rule-based system helps identify high-priority requests.

03
Help is Assigned

Suitable volunteers or NGOs can accept requests and coordinate available resources.

04
Track Resolution

Citizens can track progress from reported to assigned, in-progress and resolved.
